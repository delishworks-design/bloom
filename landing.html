What's in the app

Small tools. One pattern.

Everything below works without a signal. Sign in only if you want your own devices to share the same history.

  1. 01
    Cycle & predictionLog a start. Three of them make an average you can lean on.
  2. 02
    Daily logWater, mood, sleep, energy, symptoms, meals. One tap each.
  3. 03
    PlannerTasks and notes for today, with yesterday still within reach.
  4. 04
    Bloom GardenEight little gardens grow from what you actually log.
  5. 05
    LibrarySave books for offline reading and keep a minutes streak.
  6. 06
    Partner viewAn 8-character code gives someone a read-only window.
  7. 07
    SyncReal-time updates between your own phones, when you enable it.
  8. 08
    Widgets & nudgesWater, mood and tasks on the home screen. One reminders toggle.

That is the number of logged period starts Bloom needs before its prediction stops guessing.

Every start after that sharpens it. A missed day does not reset anything.

How it starts

No setup wizard.

  1. Open the app

    Start on this phone, offline, with no email and no waiting.

  2. Log the first day

    One date is enough to begin. Add water or a mood if today calls for it.

  3. Let the pattern appear

    After three starts, cycle length and insights become something you can trust.

  4. Invite someone, if you want

    Share a code for a read-only view, or sign in to sync your own devices.
