What's in the app

What you get.

None of this needs a signal. Your logs stay on this phone.

  1. 01
    Cycle & predictionThree starts give you a real average. Every start after that makes it steadier.
  2. 02
    Daily logWater, mood, sleep, energy, symptoms, meals. One tap each.
  3. 03
    PlannerToday's tasks and notes. Yesterday is still there when you need it.
  4. 04
    Bloom GardenEight gardens that grow from your own logs. No streak guilt.
  5. 05
    LibraryBooks you can read offline. Minutes add up to a small garden.
  6. 06
    Partner viewAn eight-character code. They can look, they cannot edit.
  7. 07
    SyncYour own phones, updated as you log. Your call.
  8. 08
    Widgets & nudgesWater, mood and tasks on the home screen. One reminders toggle, no settings maze.

Every start after that sharpens it. Miss a day and nothing resets.

How it starts

No setup wizard.

  1. Log the first day

    One date is enough. Add water or a mood if today calls for it.

  2. Let the pattern appear

    Three starts and the average is worth trusting. Insights follow.

  3. Invite someone, if you want

    A code gives someone a read-only view. Your own phones sync when you want them to.
